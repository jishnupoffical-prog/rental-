## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the intersection of architectural luxury and cutting-edge hardware capability. Crafted for creative professionals, computational engineers, digital nomads, and elite gamers seeking short-term access to top-tier hardware without compromise.

The emotional core is defined by quiet power, surgical precision, and effortless prestige. Rather than traditional aggressive gaming tropes or sterile corporate aesthetics, the visual language draws heavily from **Obsidian Glassmorphism** blended with **Minimalist High-Tech Luxury**. It pairs deep obsidian canvas tiers with brushed-graphite structural layers, illuminated by whisper-thin champagne gold borders and high-energy electric cyan telemetry accents. Surfaces evoke dark smoked glass, metallic chamfers, and deep optical depth.

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for deep-dark displays, optimizing contrast ratios while preserving midnight fidelity.

- **Primary (`#00F0FF` - Electric Cyan):** The pulse of modern computation. Reserved for interactive signals, active states, key specs, selection checkmarks, and dynamic pricing metrics.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37` - Champagne Gold):** Denotes top-tier luxury, premium badge borders, VIP reservation highlights, and subtle rating emblems.
- **Tertiary (`#1B222C` - Slate Graphite):** Forms mid-level structural cards, surface borders, and elevated controls that sit directly atop deep obsidian backdrops.
- **Neutral Base (`#0A0D12` - Deep Obsidian):** The foundational infinite canvas, paired with `#12171F` for elevated card fills and `#E6EDF5` for crisp, glare-free optical body text.

Functional status tokens complement the palette: `#00E676` for immediate hardware availability (`TRUE`), `#FF3D71` for maintenance or fully booked units, and `#FFB300` for pending rental approval.

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional dialogue between contemporary editorial refinement and technical clarity. 

- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Crafted with balanced geometric curves and tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`), giving gadget names, rental tiers, and landing headers a high-end luxury tech stance.
- **Body & Content (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral, and structurally sound at all scales, ensuring long hardware descriptions, rental agreements, and invoice terms remain comfortable during prolonged reading.
- **Telemetry & Hardware Specifications (JetBrains Mono):** Hardware metrics (e.g., `RTX 4050`, `8GB RAM`, `500GB SSD`, `ID #912`, timestamps, and pricing units like `$100 / hr`) employ monospaced sizing to deliver an authentic, high-precision instrument feel.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an 8-point base rhythm, relying on a 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (`1440px` target max-width), an 8-column grid on tablet, and a 4-column flow on mobile viewports.

Hardware listings reflow dynamically:
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 3-column or 4-column gadget grids with fixed sticky booking summary rail on detail flows.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 2-column gadget cards, booking summary transitions to an anchored bottom glass dock.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column stacked cards with horizontal scrolling pill carousels for category filtering and hardware specs.

Outer margins ensure elements never touch device bezels, breathing ample space around premium hardware imagery.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is achieved through smoked translucent planes, subtle physical lighting, and ambient neon reflections rather than stark shadows:

- **Surface 0 (Canvas):** Base background tinted in solid `#0A0D12` with a subtle radial gradient of `#00F0FF0A` anchored at the top hero plane.
- **Surface 1 (Card & Section Containers):** Smoked obsidian backdrop blur (`rgba(18, 23, 31, 0.72)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), encased in a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Surface 2 (Elevated Hover & Modals):** Semi-opaque dark slate fill (`rgba(27, 34, 44, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`), framed with a glowing top-edge highlight (`1px solid rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)`).
- **Surface 3 (Floating Rental Drawer & Tooltips):** Deep obsidian glass with an ambient multi-stop drop shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 24px -2px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Active Accents:** Active cards project an ethereal, diffused cyan floor glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15)`), giving the impression of energized internal circuits.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts modern architectural geometry (`roundedness: 2`). Standard interactive elements utilize `8px` (`0.5rem`) radiuses, cards and containers balance at `16px` (`1rem`), and primary modal shells resolve at `24px` (`1.5rem`).

High-density technical chips, availability indicators, and hardware specification tags deviate into continuous full-pill geometry (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to contrast against the sharp planar boundaries of the surrounding hardware display cards.

## Components

### Hardware Rental Cards
- Built on Surface 1 glass with an internal 16:10 hardware presentation window.
- The top-right badge displays the availability indicator: an emerald pulsing dot paired with `AVAILABLE` or a muted amber dot for `IN USE`.
- The bottom cluster highlights the gadget title (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), category subtitle (`Laptop / High-Performance`), and price token.
- Includes a spec pill bar featuring hardware tags (e.g., `RTX 4050 2GB`, `8GB RAM`, `500GB NVMe`).

### Price Badges
- Features a high-contrast container with monospaced currency typography (`$100`).
- Accompanied by a muted label (`/ hr` or `/ day`) set in `JetBrains Mono`.
- Premium or flagship tier machines feature an optional hairline border in Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`) with a subtle metallic reflection.

### Specification Pills (Spec Pills)
- Full-pill geometry (`border-radius: 9999px`) with a micro-padding footprint (`4px 10px`).
- Filled with dark translucent slate (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) and a delicate `1px` stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
- Typography set in `spec-code` (`JetBrains Mono`, 12px), highlighting technical attributes like `GPU: RTX 4050`, `RAM: 8GB`, and `STORAGE: 500GB`.

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`) with deep obsidian typography (`#0A0D12`, `Plus Jakarta Sans 600`). On hover, emits a focused `0 0 20px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)` glow.
- **Secondary / Glass:** Smoked slate background with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` border and light text. On hover, the border shifts to Electric Cyan with an internal brightening tint.
- **Ghost:** Borderless, neutral slate text transitioning to champagne gold or cyan text on interaction.

### Form Inputs & Date/Time Selectors
- Background filled with dark graphite (`#12171F`), finished with an inset shadow and `1px` border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
- Focus state brings a sharp transition to `1px solid #00F0FF` accompanied by a 2px outer cyan ring blur.
- Rental duration inputs (Start Date, End Date, Rental Hours) display real-time price calculators dynamically calculated via monospaced counters.

### Rental Checkout / Schedule Bar
- A persistent bottom or side glassmorphic tray summarizing selected Gadget ID, duration, phone verification status, and real-time total calculation.
- Features a dual-action trigger: `Instant Reserve` (Cyan) and `Save Quote` (Ghost/Slate).
